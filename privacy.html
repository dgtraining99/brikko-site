Privacy policy

Brikko Games and Brikko Studio Tools · last updated 8 October 2026

Who we are

Brikko is a small studio that makes games on Roblox and posts short videos about them on YouTube and Instagram. Contact: dgtraining99@gmail.com.

Brikko Studio Tools (our internal app)

Brikko Studio Tools is a private tool used only by the Brikko team, signed in with Brikko's own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else. With our own authorization it uses:

  • YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API: to read statistics of our own channels (views, watch time, traffic sources, country totals) and to edit the titles and descriptions of our own videos.
  • Instagram Graph API: to read statistics of our own Instagram account and its posts (reach, views, likes, shares, saves, audience totals).

The data is aggregated statistics about our own content. It does not include personal information about viewers; the platforms only provide totals. It is stored on the Brikko team's own computer and in our private project records, used only to decide which videos and games to make, and never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access tokens stay on that computer and can be revoked at any time in the Google Account (Third-party access) or in Meta's business settings.

Brikko Studio Tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is accessed under the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.

Our Roblox games

Our games run on Roblox. We do not ask players for personal information. Roblox processes account data under the Roblox Privacy Policy. Inside our games we save only game progress (such as coins and items) with Roblox's own storage, and we see only aggregated statistics (for example the number of daily players) that Roblox provides to creators.

Children

Our games and videos are made for a young audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we update this page and its date. Questions or deletion requests: dgtraining99@gmail.com.
