Privacy policy

Brikko Games and Brikko Studio Tools · last updated 8 October 2026

Who we are

Brikko is a small studio that makes games on Roblox and posts short videos about them on YouTube and Instagram. Contact: dgtraining99@gmail.com.

Brikko Studio Tools (our internal app)

Brikko Studio Tools is a private tool used only by the Brikko team, signed in with Brikko's own accounts. It is not offered to anyone else. Brikko Studio Tools uses YouTube API Services. With our own authorization it uses:

  • YouTube Data API: to upload and schedule our own videos (Shorts) to our own channels, set their title, description, publish time and "made for kids" setting, edit the titles and descriptions of our own videos, and read the list of our own videos.
  • YouTube Analytics API: to read statistics of our own channels (views, watch time, traffic sources, country totals).
  • Instagram Graph API: to publish our own Reels and read statistics of our own Instagram account and its posts (reach, views, likes, shares, saves, audience totals).

What information is accessed, stored and shared

  • Accessed: our own channels' video list, video settings and aggregated statistics. No personal information about viewers: the platforms only provide totals.
  • Stored: the sign-in tokens (on the Brikko team's own computer only) and short statistics reports about our own videos (in our private project records). Statistics are refreshed daily. Reports are kept for at most 36 months, then deleted.
  • Used: only to publish our own videos and to decide which videos and games to make.
  • Shared: never. The data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for advertising. Brikko Studio Tools shows no advertising and lets no third party serve content.
  • Cookies and device storage: Brikko Studio Tools is a command-line tool. It sets no cookies; it stores only its sign-in tokens in files on the Brikko team's computer. This website sets no cookies.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access to a Google account can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions; Instagram access can be removed in Meta's business settings. After access is revoked we delete the stored data from that account within 30 days. To ask for deletion, email dgtraining99@gmail.com; we delete the data within 7 days.

Brikko Studio Tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. By using Brikko Studio Tools, users agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Our Roblox games

Our games run on Roblox. We do not ask players for personal information. Roblox processes account data under the Roblox Privacy Policy. Inside our games we save only game progress (such as coins and items) with Roblox's own storage, and we see only aggregated statistics (for example the number of daily players) that Roblox provides to creators.

Children

Our games and videos are made for a young audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we update this page and its date. Questions or deletion requests: dgtraining99@gmail.com.
