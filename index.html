Brikko Studio Tools

Brikko Studio Tools is our internal software. It uses YouTube API Services to upload and schedule our own Shorts on our own YouTube channels and to read their statistics (views, watch time, traffic sources), and the Instagram Graph API to publish our own Reels and read their statistics, so we can see which videos work. It is used only by the Brikko team for Brikko's own accounts; it is not offered to the public. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See our privacy policy and terms.
